Immigration Guidelines

Are You Eligible to Hire a Domestic Helper?

The Malaysian Immigration Department requires employers to meet specific household income and care justification standards. Here is what is needed:

Household Income Standards

Official income thresholds set by Malaysian Immigration:

  • Indonesian Helper: Minimum joint household income of RM3,000 to RM5,000 / month
  • Filipino Helper: Minimum joint household income of RM5,000 / month
  • Husband & wife incomes can be combined with marriage certificate

Accepted Justifications

Immigration grants helper permits based on valid home needs:

  • Caring for young children or infants (under 15 years old)
  • Assisting elderly or bedridden parents (medical letter if required)
  • Maintaining large residence or multi-generational households

Required Documents

Simple documentation prepared for our submission:

  • Copy of Employer NRIC (MyKad) and spouse NRIC
  • Marriage Certificate & Children's Birth Certificates
  • Latest Form BE / EA, EPF statement or 3 months bank slips

Common Inquiries

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear, honest answers about hiring domestic helpers, visa timelines, FOMEMA medical checks, and immigration criteria.

Is Agensi Pekerjaan Nasav Global fully licensed in Malaysia?

Yes. Agensi Pekerjaan Nasav Global Sdn Bhd is a legally registered and licensed employment agency under the Private Employment Agencies Act 1981, authorized by the Ministry of Human Resources (JTKSM), Companies Commission of Malaysia (SSM), and Jabatan Imigresen Malaysia. We operate strictly in compliance with bilateral labor agreements.

How long does the entire hiring process take from selection to arrival?

The standard hiring journey typically takes 8 to 12 weeks. This duration encompasses source country medical checkups, passport documentation, Malaysian Immigration Calling Visa (VDR) endorsement, embassy clearance, and international flight arrangements.

What happens if the helper fails the FOMEMA medical check-up in Malaysia?

All candidates undergo strict pre-departure screening in their home country to prevent issues. However, if a worker fails the mandatory FOMEMA screening upon arrival in Malaysia, Malaysian law requires immediate repatriation. Under Nasav Global's agency agreement, employers will receive a replacement candidate or refund in accordance with contract terms.

Can I interview the shortlisted candidate before making a decision?

Yes, absolutely! We encourage virtual video interviews via WhatsApp or Zoom. This allows employers to speak directly with the candidate, assess communication skills, evaluate work chemistry, and explain unique household routines before formal commitment.

What are the minimum salary and income requirements for Malaysian employers?

Under Immigration Department regulations, the employer's joint household income must generally be at least RM3,000 to RM5,000 monthly for an Indonesian helper, and RM5,000 for a Filipino helper. The basic monthly helper wage is set at RM1,500 (Indonesia MOU) or USD 400 equivalent (~RM1,680 for Philippines).

How does Nasav Global support families if adjustment issues occur?

We provide ongoing bilingual counselling services. If communication barriers or cultural adjustment issues arise, our team speaks with both the employer and helper to resolve expectations. If compatibility cannot be achieved within the agreement warranty, we provide free replacement facilitation.
