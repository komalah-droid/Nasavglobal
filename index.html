From Selection to Arrival

We handle everything from candidate screening to airport handover, keeping you informed at every single milestone.

1
Stage 01 • Day 1

Family Needs Consultation

We assess your family's routines, dietary guidelines, elderly medical conditions, or children's age groups to craft a tailored candidate requirement profile.

2
Stage 02 • Days 2–5

Candidate Biodata & Video Interview

Review screened helper profiles with complete history, skill assessments, and conduct live video interviews with candidates to ensure mutual compatibility.

3
Stage 03 • Weeks 1–3

Origin Country Medical & Security Clearance

The chosen helper undergoes comprehensive health screening, police record verification, passport processing, and government training in their home country.

4
Stage 04 • Weeks 4–7

Malaysian Immigration Calling Visa (VDR)

Nasav Global submits employer documentation to the Malaysian Immigration Department (e-VDR) for official entry approval and work permit sanction.

5
Stage 05 • Weeks 8–10

Flight Booking & Airport Reception

We coordinate flight booking and personally receive the helper at KLIA. Our team conducts an arrival briefing and handles transportation to our orientation center.

6
Stage 06 • Days 1–3 in Malaysia

FOMEMA Medical & Immigration Endorsement

The helper completes mandatory FOMEMA medical check-up in Malaysia, SOCSO insurance activation, and biometric permit endorsement with Immigration.

7
Stage 07 • Ongoing Support

Safe Home Handover & 365-Day Counselling

We introduce the helper to your household with employment contract signing, orientation guidelines, and continuous bilingual counselling support for a harmonious home.
