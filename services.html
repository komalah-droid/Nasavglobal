Regulatory & Documentation

Visa, Work Permit & Immigration Services

Skip the tedious queues at Putrajaya Immigration. Nasav Global handles the entire regulatory spectrum with authorized Malaysian government portals.

Calling Visa (e-VDR)

Processing electronic Visa with Reference (VDR) approvals through the Malaysian Immigration portal in accordance with Ministry of Human Resources requirements.

Work Permit Renewals

Annual PLKS permit renewals via MYEG before expiration to avoid compound fines or illegal overstay status for your domestic helper.

FOMEMA Medical Checkups

Coordinating Year 1, 2, and 3 mandatory medical examinations (blood tests, chest X-rays, physical screening) at accredited FOMEMA panel clinics.

SOCSO & Insurance

Ensuring compulsory Foreign Worker Employment Injury Scheme (SOCSO) enrollment and comprehensive medical insurance coverage.

Transfer of Employer

Legal transfer of maid sponsorship between authorized employers under Immigration Department guidelines and release letters.

Check-Out Memo (COM)

Managing contract completion check-out memo processing with Immigration, airport escort, and security bond release.
